## SECTION 02 — THE THESIS (positioning paragraph)

### Pull-quote
**Modernise — but keep it human.**

### Body
The temptation, when a business gets busier, is to bolt on more tools. More plugins. Another platform. Another freelancer. A few months in, the founder is the only one who knows how any of it fits together.

We do the opposite. We strip the stack back to what's working, pick the right tool for the next phase, and design every customer touch-point — site, email, booking, follow-up — so it actually feels like one business. The kind of experience that holds up whether someone arrives at 7am on their phone or sits with you for an hour on a call.

The technology is modern. The work is still human.

## SECTION 04 — HOW WE WORK (process strip, four steps)

**01 · Listen**
A short call. We map what you've got, what's leaking and what next quarter actually needs to look like.

**02 · Scope**
A clear written proposal. Fixed-fee phases, named deliverables, dates we both believe in.

**03 · Build**
Design and development in short, reviewable chunks — with a Loom walkthrough at every milestone so you're never wondering where things are at.

**04 · Hand over (or stay on)**
Documented handover, a recorded walkthrough of every system, and the option of a monthly platform audit if you want a second pair of eyes on it forever.
