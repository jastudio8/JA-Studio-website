## Positioning at a glance

**One-line:** A small studio building modern, human websites and customer journeys for service providers.

**The thesis:** Most service businesses don't need more software — they need a calmer system that actually fits how they work. Modern stack, considered design, automation where it earns its place, humans on every other line.

**The stack we build on:** Shopify · Webflow · WordPress · Netlify · Klaviyo · Cal.com · Stripe · Xero · Asana · Claude

**Who it's for:**
- Coaches, naturopaths, retreat hosts, ecommerce founders, lifestyle brands
- 1–10 person teams that have outgrown DIY but don't want a faceless agency
- Founders who care about how the work *feels* — not just the deliverable

**Proof:** Hayls & Kyle · Live Well Movement · Custom Made Coaching · AJ Health · The Booty Movement · The Basic Co · Fitventure Retreats

## SEO notes (for build phase)

- Title tag pattern: `[Page] — Jess Anderson Studio | Websites & Customer Journeys for Service Providers`
- Primary keywords: Shopify designer Australia, Klaviyo email designer, customer journey design, website designer for coaches, Webflow designer Brisbane/Gold Coast
- Schema.org: `LocalBusiness` + `ProfessionalService`, with `areaServed: Australia` and `knowsAbout` listing the stack
- Every case study page = `Article` schema with `image`, `datePublished`, `author: Jess Anderson Studio`, `mentions` of the client business

## Voice rules (so future edits stay consistent)

1. Sentences end where they need to. No filler.
2. Use plain words when a plain word will do. ("Built," not "engineered.")
3. Talk about the client, not the studio. The studio shows up in the verbs.
4. No "passion" / "synergy" / "elevate" — say what was actually done.
5. Australian spelling throughout (modernise, organise, optimise, colour, behaviour).
