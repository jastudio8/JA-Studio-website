## SECTION 04 — FAQ (short)

**Do you work with new businesses?**
Yes. Some of our favourite projects have been pre-launch. The brief stays the same: design for the next phase, not the one you've already outgrown.

**Are you taking on monthly retainer clients?**
Sometimes. The studio is small on purpose, so retainers open and close depending on capacity. Best way to find out: book a call.

**Do you work on-site / in person?**
Mostly remote. Workshops, kick-offs and strategy days can be done in person depending on where you are.

**Do you only work with women-led businesses?**
No — though a lot of our clients happen to be. We work with people we like, regardless.

**Can you take over an existing site?**
Often, yes. We've inherited builds from Kartra, Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, Webflow and various in-house developers. We'll tell you straight if a rebuild is faster than a rescue.
