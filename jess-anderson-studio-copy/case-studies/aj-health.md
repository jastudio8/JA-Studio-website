## OVERVIEW

Annie is a degree-qualified naturopath, educator and co-founder of Nunie, a microbiome-safe intimacy brand. Her clinical practice runs entirely online, across Australia — supporting women through gut health, female hormones, perimenopause, sexual health, mental health and functional testing.

The brief was clear: a site that finally reads the way Annie actually practises — degree-trained, evidence-led, warm — and a booking system that gets people into her diary without friction.

---

## WHAT WAS GOING ON

- A previous site that didn't carry Annie's voice or her clinical authority
- A booking journey that took multiple clicks across multiple platforms
- A bank of case studies and testimonials that wasn't being put to work
- A second brand (Nunie) that needed a clean handoff *from* the practitioner site without competing with it

---

## WHAT WE BUILT

### A clinical home
A WordPress site designed around the way patients actually arrive — by symptom (gut, hormones, perimenopause, mental health), by service, or via the case studies that look most like their own story.

### Six service pillars, properly visualised
- Microbiome Health · Sexual Health · Endometriosis & PCOS · Perimenopause & Menopause · Mental Health · Bloating & Gut Issues
Each with its own page, its own micro-funnel and its own enquiry pathway.

### A root-cause framework, written down
Five principles — *Personalised Care · Functional Testing · Integrative Approach · Nutrition & Lifestyle · Long-Term Strategy · Empowerment Through Education* — that now hold every service and case study together.

### Booking + intake
Direct integration with Simple Clinic Online for diary booking. Discovery-call funnel for new patients. Klaviyo welcome series for the in-between.

### Workshops & talks
A dedicated section for in-person workshops — *Healing, but with Friends* — with a simple enquiry-to-hosting flow.

### Nunie handoff
A clean, opinionated link to Nunie sits in the right place on the site — visible, on-brand, but never competing with the clinical work.

---

## OUTCOMES

- Annie sounds like Annie. Patients arrive having already done half the work of trusting her.
- Three live case studies (Amelia · Sarah K · the 56-year-old female cohort) now do measurable lifting on conversion
- Bookings route through one system, with the option to add a discovery call when the calendar gets tight
