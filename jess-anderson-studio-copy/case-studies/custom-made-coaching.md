## OVERVIEW

Custom Made Coaching is a coaching business with a serious roster — from people doing their first squat at home, to lifters preparing to step on stage. The challenge was that all four tiers were carrying different design treatments, different checkout journeys and different post-sign-up experiences. Members were getting through, but the brand wasn't.

We rebuilt the site around a single, clean architecture — *Higher Self · Build a better you* — that holds all four offerings without flattening what makes each one different.

---

## WHAT WAS GOING ON

- Four programs (Kickstart, Stronger, Train Like a Pro, 1-1 Coaching) with inconsistent design and pricing display
- A blog and freebie library full of useful content that wasn't being found
- Lead-magnet eBooks (CMC Recipes, MyFitnessPal guide) sitting outside the email system
- A "Results" page that was the strongest part of the brand — and wasn't being used as proof

---

## WHAT WE BUILT

### A four-tier shop, properly priced
- **Kickstart** — $25/week, home + gym options, beginner-friendly
- **Stronger** — $35/week, for the lifter ready to level up
- **Train Like a Pro** — $55/week, elite-level training systems
- **1-1 Coaching** — application-based, for real life changes and stage prep
- 7-day free trials wired through every tier where it made sense

### A "Results" wall
The transformation gallery now sits high on the homepage — real names, real photos, real numbers — and threads through every program page as proof.

### Learning hub
The CMC blog rebuilt as a learning hub — *Why your log book matters · Tempo and form · Importance of warm-up sets · From overwhelmed to organised* — each piece designed to do double duty as SEO and as nurture-flow content.

### Freebies + email
The Free CMC Recipes and MyFitnessPal eBooks each got their own opt-in page, Klaviyo flow and follow-up sequence — so a free download becomes the start of a relationship, not a dead-end PDF.

---

## OUTCOMES

- One coherent shop replacing four scattered program pages
- A blog and freebie library that finally connects to the customer journey instead of sitting beside it
- A site the team can update themselves through Elementor — without breaking the design

---

## QUIET DETAILS

- WordPress auto-updates configured through SiteGround, so security patches don't need a phone call
- Inode quota monitoring set up to catch hosting issues before they hit the front of the site
- Image optimisation pass that brought the home page hero-load under 2 seconds on mobile
