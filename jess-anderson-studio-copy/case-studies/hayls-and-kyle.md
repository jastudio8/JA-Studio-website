## OVERVIEW

Hayley and Kyle have built a real community across socials and Patreon — the kind of audience that turns up week after week. But the website was carrying maybe a third of that story, and the most committed part of their audience (their Patreon members) wasn't visible anywhere on the site.

We were brought in to take a hard look at what the site was actually doing for them, and to design the next phase around the way the business actually works.

---

## WHAT WAS GOING ON

- A Shopify site doing okay on product but missing the rest of the picture
- Patreon — the most lucrative tier of the business — nowhere on the homepage
- A growing audience landing on the site and not knowing where to go next
- An audit that confirmed what they already suspected: the brand had outgrown the build

---

## WHAT WE BUILT

### Audit & rewrite
A site audit walked Hayley and Kyle through what the data was saying — where people were dropping, what they were searching for, what the site wasn't doing. The audit ended with two recommended paths: a focused conversion-lift on the existing site, or a full rebuild around three pillars (Patreon · Shop · Stories).

### Brand & system
A refreshed visual system designed to carry both the product side (Shopify) and the community side (Patreon, member-only content, the YouTube + podcast ecosystem they already have).

### Site architecture
- A homepage that finally surfaces Patreon as a first-class destination
- Shopify storefront optimised for the way their audience actually shops
- Content hub that pulls weekly stories into the site rather than leaving them on socials
- Klaviyo set-up tied to both the shop and the member tier

---

## OUTCOMES (in progress)

- Two new directions presented, each priced, each scoped — so Hayley and Kyle could choose with full information
- The audit alone made the Patreon gap impossible to ignore — and is now the centrepiece of the next phase
- A working partnership with the kind of clients who want decisions made *with* them, not *to* them

---

## QUIET DETAILS

- The audit was delivered as a written doc *and* a Loom — Hayley walked Kyle through the Loom rather than re-explaining it
- The brand work is being staged: refresh first, full rebuild as Phase 2, so cashflow stays sensible
- Shopify billing, third-party app costs and Klaviyo profile limits all got a clean-up at the same time
