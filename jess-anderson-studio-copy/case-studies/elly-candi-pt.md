## OVERVIEW

Elly Candi has spent fifteen years coaching women toward strength — 500+ clients, 3,000+ sessions, and one unfashionable message: most women are under-eating, over-thinking and under-recovering, and you don't need extremes to see results. Her flagship is RESHAPE, a 16-week body-recomposition program, sitting alongside 1:1 personal training and online coaching. The coaching was already proven. What it needed was a home that sold the calm, evidence-led approach as clearly as the results her clients post.

---

## WHAT WAS GOING ON

- A booked-out coach whose best proof — strength gains, post-natal comebacks, real body change — lived mostly in Instagram DMs
- A flagship 16-week program (RESHAPE) that deserved its own clear, scannable page rather than a link in bio
- Free resources (glute guide, warm-up and mobility guide, habit tracker) being handed out with no system behind them
- Enquiries arriving across forms, comments and DMs, with no single place they landed

---

## WHAT WE BUILT

### A Shopify home for women's wellbeing
A Shopify store that frames the whole offer — RESHAPE, 1:1 training and online coaching — in plain, scannable detail, so a first-time visitor understands the approach before they understand the price.

### Free guides as the real front door
The glute guide, warm-up and mobility guide and habit tracker set up as genuine lead magnets — a name and an email in exchange for something useful — each one tagged on the way in so Elly knows what a new lead actually wants.

### One enquiry pipeline
Typeform intake collecting the right detail up front (goals, history, situation), routed so every enquiry lands in one place instead of being scattered across DMs and comments.

### Instagram, automated where it earns it
ManyChat catching the Instagram replies that start most of Elly's conversations — keyword to guide, comment to link — without losing the human follow-up.

---

## OUTCOMES

- A site Elly can send anyone to — first reel to first session running as one journey, not a scatter of links
- RESHAPE with a home of its own, sold on the method as much as the results
- A free-resource funnel quietly building a warm list between launches

---

## QUIET DETAILS

- The free guides do the quiet work — each one tagged on the way in so Elly knows what a new lead wants before she writes the first reply
- Copy written to match how Elly already talks to clients: strength over shrinking, recovery over extremes
- Built to stay simple — one platform, one inbox, one point of contact
