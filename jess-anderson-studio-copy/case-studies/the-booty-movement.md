## OVERVIEW

Aimee Fischer-Gray (@aimefit) built The Booty Movement around a single, focused idea: strength training, glute-led, for women who want a real plan and a real coach. The site needed to do three things at once — sell three different programs, hold a members area, and feel like the coach behind it.

---

## WHAT WAS GOING ON

- A growing audience landing on the site without a clear path to the right program
- Three programs (Booty Prep · Booty Build · Booty Gains) plus a bundle, all needing distinct pages
- A lead-magnet eBook (the High Protein Recipe Pack) doing nothing for the email list
- A Q2 newsletter program that needed to actually run

---

## WHAT WE BUILT

### Three programs, one shop
- **Booty Prep** — beginner-focused, technique-led, glute activation
- **Booty Build** — 12-week intermediate, intentional splits
- **Booty Gains** — advanced training for consistent lifters
- **The Booty Bundle** — full access, the complete journey

Each program got its own page, its own image story and its own conversion logic — without losing the through-line.

### The "find your fit" quiz
A three-minute quiz that puts a new visitor into the right program for their goals, fitness level and training style — and into the matching Klaviyo segment. The quiz pulls double-duty as a lead magnet and a routing tool.

### Recipe pack funnel
The High Protein Recipe Pack now has its own opt-in page, a Klaviyo welcome series, and a clear next step into Booty Prep.

### Members area
A logged-in members hub with each program's content, training videos and the master training library.

### Newsletter program
A Q2 newsletter calendar — twelve emails — built around the existing blog content (*Build Lean Muscle Tone · 5 fundamental movement patterns · Activate that booty*) so Aimee writes once and the system carries it.

---

## OUTCOMES

- One site doing the work of three program landing pages, a members area and a newsletter engine
- A lead magnet that finally feeds the list it was always supposed to
- The "find your fit" quiz turning cold scrollers into the right kind of buyer
