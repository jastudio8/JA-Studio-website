## OVERVIEW

Tristan Brown is a movement coach and mentor whose work is hard to put in a box — part strength training, part stillness, part the conversations a man has when he finally stops outrunning himself. The practice was strong. The way it showed up online wasn't.

We were brought in to do two things at once: design a home for the practice that *read like Tristan*, and build the customer journey that would let it scale beyond his calendar.

---

## WHAT WAS GOING ON

- A first-pass site that didn't carry his voice
- Mentorship enquiries living in DMs, with no system behind them
- A new "5-Day Embodiment Challenge" that needed a home, a funnel and a flow
- A 12-Session Ideal Self Mentorship offer that needed positioning before it could be sold

---

## WHAT WE BUILT

### Phase 1 — The site
A new Webflow site, designed around the three ways someone can work with Tristan: the free 5-day challenge, the movement-focused mentorship, and the full 12-session journey. Editorial layout, real photography, language pulled from long-form conversations with him rather than written for SEO.

The "Our Why" page exists because Tristan asked, *"Where do people read the actual point of all this?"* It now sits up the top of the nav, beside About, exactly where he wanted it.

### Phase 2 — The journey
- **Cal.com booking embed** — for the free discovery call, routed straight into Tristan's calendar and a Klaviyo segment
- **5-Day Embodiment Challenge funnel** — opt-in page, daily-email sequence, a clear next step on day five
- **Mentorship enquiry flow** — different content for free-challenge graduates vs. cold leads
- **Klaviyo welcome series, abandoned cart, post-purchase and field-notes weekly newsletter**

### Phase 3 — Stories
A blog (he calls it *Field Notes* and *Stories from the Practice*) that's plugged into the email program. Every Field Note is a newsletter and a journal entry at once — written long-form by Tristan, finished in the site by us.

---

## OUTCOMES

- **Free challenge live and converting** — the opt-in funnel and Klaviyo flow are running, with discovery-call bookings landing in Tristan's inbox within 24 hours of sign-up
- **A mentorship practice with a back-end** — bookings, intake, follow-up and content all routed through one Klaviyo, one Cal.com, one Webflow
- **A site that holds his voice** — long-form, image-led, designed to be sat with rather than scrolled past

---

## QUIET DETAILS

- A site restore from Webflow's backup, used once mid-project, took 8 minutes and zero panic
- Phase 2 was scoped and quoted *after* Phase 1 launched — so Tristan saw exactly what he was paying for
- A monthly platform audit now keeps Klaviyo deliverability, flow health and site speed quietly in line
