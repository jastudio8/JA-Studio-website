## OVERVIEW

Elly and Kiala run women's fitness retreats on Hawai'i's North Shore — beachside bootcamps, guided hikes, shark dives, content workshops, and the kind of week that ends with everyone in tears at the airport. The product was already extraordinary. What it needed was a site that *looked* like the trip.

---

## WHAT WAS GOING ON

- A retreat business growing on word-of-mouth, needing a digital front-door that matched
- A partnership pipeline (brands, sponsors, content collaborators) without a clear application path
- A waitlist that was building but had no system behind it
- A second retreat (Morocco) coming, with no obvious place to announce it

---

## WHAT WE BUILT

### A site that feels like the trip
A custom-feeling site built on Webflow, anchored by hero video (sunrise training, ocean, hikes), with a five-step "experience" walkthrough — *Workout with Elly · Diving and Snorkelling · Guided Hikes · Content Creation with Kiala · Recovery and Relaxation*.

### Two faces, two stories
- **Meet Elly** — your coach and hype girl, 15 years coaching, 3,000+ sessions
- **Meet Kiala** — Hawai'i-based content creator, Harvard nutrition certs, social media playbook
Each gets their own image and bio, framed so the reader knows immediately whose hand they're shaking when they show up at the villa.

### Retreat package, plainly told
What you actually get — North Shore villa, 7 nights, all meals, private chef, daily sessions, all levels, shark dives, snorkel tours, surf lessons, lei making, sunset cruise — written in plain English, broken into clear, scannable cards.

### Enquiry pipeline
A Google Form embedded into the funnel (with intake survey for retreat fit), routed into Elly and Kiala's inbox and tagged into Klaviyo for nurture.

### Partnership funnel
A dedicated `/partnerships` page for brands and sponsors who want to apply to partner — a separate enquiry, separate brief, separate inbox.

### Coming-soon Morocco
The Morocco retreat lives in the nav as *coming soon* — letting Elly and Kiala build a waitlist before the offer is finished.

---

## OUTCOMES

- A site Elly was comfortable putting in front of premium hotel partners and Hawaii-based brands
- A working enquiry pipeline — from "I saw your reel" to "I'm holding a deposit" — running on one form
- The Morocco waitlist quietly building without the pressure of a launch

---

## QUIET DETAILS

- Galleries built with infinite-scroll image strips to mimic Instagram — meeting the audience where they already are
- FAQ section written verbatim from the questions Elly was already getting in DMs
- All hero video served via CDN so a 4K opening doesn't wreck the mobile LCP
