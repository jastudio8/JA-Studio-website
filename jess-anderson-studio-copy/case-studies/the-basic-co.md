## OVERVIEW

The Basic Co is an Australian-owned ecommerce brand for everyday wellness — Australian-made, women-led, sustainability-driven, ethical and cruelty-free. Hannah also runs Shique Hair & Beauty, a salon brand servicing weddings, fitness competitions and full-service hair and beauty across South-East Queensland.

The two businesses had grown up on different platforms (Shopify and Kartra), with two Klaviyo accounts, two customer records and two sets of flows. They were running, but at the cost of everything Hannah's calendar.

We were brought in to merge them — quietly, without dropping deliverability, without losing the warmth that's built the brand.

---

## WHAT WAS GOING ON

- Two Klaviyo accounts, recently merged, with 59 flows (many legacy fitness-comp flows from 2022–24 still marked "live")
- 95% of the active Klaviyo profile limit reached — list hygiene urgent
- Shique Hair & Beauty still on Kartra, needing to migrate to Shopify
- Email open rates below industry benchmark
- 40+ draft "template" campaigns cluttering the dashboard post-merge
- Easter and AfterPay Day campaigns landing well, but as one-offs rather than a system

---

## WHAT WE BUILT (and continue to)

### Klaviyo clean-up
- Re-engagement campaign — *Still want emails from us?* — to the unengaged 90-day segment
- Suppression of profiles who'd received 5+ emails and opened zero in 90 days, dropping list cost and lifting deliverability in the same week
- Three new core segments — *Engaged 30d · Engaged 90d · Unengaged 90+*  — used as the new campaign default
- Sunset flow finalised from draft
- Archive of 20+ legacy fitness-comp flows tied to event lists that no longer exist

### Flow rebuilds
- TBC — Abandoned Cart (new) · Placed Order (new) · Cancelled Order (new) · Newsletter
- SHB — Welcome Series · Placed Order & Booked / Not Booked · Appointment Reminder · Wedding Reminder + Wedding Booked
- New Customer Flows (including Booking) replacing old manual processes

### Site work
- Easter Sale and AfterPay Day announcement bars across both sites
- Hero image performance pass — fixing the slow-loading, sometimes-not-rendering hero on TBC
- Brand pillar strip (Australian-Owned · Women-Led · Sustainability-Driven · Wellness-Focused · Ethical & Cruelty-Free) made permanent
- Theme governance — annual theme renames so the live theme is always current

### Kartra → Shopify migration
- Shique Hair & Beauty moving from Kartra to Shopify, keeping Weddings, Fitness Competitions, Shop, Contact and the "Now Fully Mobile" service-area messaging intact
- Book Now CTA re-routed through the Shopify bookings store post-merge

### Monthly Platform Audit
Hannah now receives a written platform audit on the first of every month, covering Klaviyo, both websites, Asana and Zapier — with the three things that need attention this week called out at the top.

---

## OUTCOMES

- Klaviyo profile limit pulled back from 95% to a healthy operating range, without losing a single customer who actually wanted to hear from the brand
- Open rates climbing back toward industry benchmark after the suppression run
- Two brands behaving like two brands again, instead of one messy spreadsheet

---

## QUIET DETAILS

- The audit doc is short on purpose — three urgent items at the top, then everything else in collapsible sections
- A *Wedding Enquiry* Zap that had been silently switched off was found in audit week one — and re-armed before the next bridal cycle
- StarShipIt continues to handle shipping notifications; we left that one alone because it was already working
