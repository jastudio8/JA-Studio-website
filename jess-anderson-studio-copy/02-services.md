## SECTION 02 — EMAIL & CUSTOMER JOURNEYS

### Lead
Klaviyo flows, segments and campaigns built around how customers actually move through the business. Welcome series, abandoned cart, post-purchase, mentorship enquiries, sunset flows — designed end to end so the inbox feels like a continuation of the site, not a different brand entirely.

### What's included
- Klaviyo account audit — flows, segments, list health, deliverability
- List hygiene and re-engagement (the unglamorous work that fixes open rates)
- Flow design and build — welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, win-back, sunset, replenishment, booking reminders
- Campaign calendar and template system, so you can send without redesigning every time
- Segments that earn their place — engaged 30, engaged 90, unengaged 90+, by product, by behaviour
- Reporting that tells you which flow is doing the work and which one to retire

### Common shapes this takes
- **Klaviyo set-up from scratch** — accounts, flows, segments, first three campaigns
- **Audit + rebuild** — for inboxes that have grown 200 flows and forgotten which 12 matter
- **Monthly retainer** — campaigns, optimisation and reporting, every month

### Recent
The Basic Co + Shique Hair & Beauty (account merge, list clean-up, abandoned cart + post-purchase rebuild) · Live Well Movement (free challenge funnel, mentorship booking flow)
